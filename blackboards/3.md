### §8.2. Review of §2 Proof: |Riem|^2 = |Ric|^2

**Step 1 (Curvature formula).** R_{abcd} = (1/4) f_{abe} f_{cde}. This is the standard
formula for a Lie group with bi-invariant metric (nabla_X Y = [X,Y]/2). CORRECT.

**Step 2 (Kretschner scalar factorization).**
|Riem|^2 = (1/16) sum_{abcdef} f_{abe} f_{abf} f_{cde} f_{cdf}
         = (1/16) sum_{ef} [sum_{ab} f_{abe} f_{abf}]^2

The key: sum_{ab} f_{abe} f_{abf} = -B_{ef}^{Killing} = C_adj delta_{ef} (for simple G
with orthonormal basis where g = -(1/C_adj)B).

**sev-4 (presentation):** BB2 writes "B_{ef} = Killing form" for sum_{ab} f_{abe} f_{abf}.
Strictly, sum_{ab} f_{abe} f_{abf} = Tr(ad_{e_e} circ ad_{e_f}) evaluated in the
ORTHONORMAL basis. For the metric g = -(1/C_adj)B, this equals C_adj delta_{ef}, which
is -B_{ef}/C_adj * C_adj = -B_{ef} only in the non-orthonormal (Killing) basis.
The square B_{ef}^2 gives the right answer either way because the sign cancels. The
intermediate naming is slightly misleading but the final identity is CORRECT.

**Step 3.** |Riem|^2 = (1/16) sum_{ef} (C_adj delta_{ef})^2 = C_adj^2 d / 16. CORRECT.

**Step 4 (Ricci).** Einstein: Ric_{ab} = (R/d) g_{ab}. R = d C_adj/4 (standard).
|Ric|^2 = R^2/d = d C_adj^2/16. CORRECT.

**Step 5 (Conclusion).** |Riem|^2 = |Ric|^2 = d C_adj^2/16. QED. CORRECT.
