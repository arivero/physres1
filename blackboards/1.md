## S3. Physical Content: What the Half-Density DOES Change

### 3.1. Propagator (off-diagonal)

\[
\tilde{K}(x,y,t) = |g(x)|^{1/4}\,K(x,y,t)\,|g(y)|^{-1/4}
\]
where \(K\) is the bare heat kernel. \(\tilde{K} \neq K\) for \(x \neq y\).

**But on-diagonal:** \(\tilde{K}(x,x,t) = K(x,x,t)\). All local traces are identical.

### 3.2. Composition (the raison d'etre)

Bare: \(\int K(x,z,s)\,K(z,y,t)\,|g(z)|^{1/2}\,d^dz = K(x,y,s+t)\).

Half-density: \(\int \tilde{K}(x,z,s)\,\tilde{K}(z,y,t)\,d^dz = \tilde{K}(x,y,s+t)\).

The half-density kernel composes WITHOUT a measure factor. This is a GLOBAL property of the propagator, not a local one.

### 3.3. Eigenfunctions

O1 eigenfunctions: \(\{f_n\}\) orthonormal in \(L^2(\mathrm{dvol}_g)\).
O2 eigenfunctions: \(\{|g|^{1/4}f_n\}\) orthonormal in \(L^2(dx)\).

The eigenFUNCTIONS differ by the position-dependent factor \(|g|^{1/4}\). The eigenVALUES are the same.

### 3.4. Inner Product and Hilbert Space

Composition in half-density form selects the \(L^2(dx)\) Hilbert space (no measure weight), while bare form uses \(L^2(\mathrm{dvol}_g)\) (geometric measure).

This is the physical content: not spectral, but KINEMATIC. The half-density normalization determines the correct inner product for path-integral composition.
